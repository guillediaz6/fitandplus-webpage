## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-octane human performance, uncompromising luxury, and dark architectural precision. Designed for an elite athletic collective and urban performance sanctuary, it merges the relentless intensity of championship athletics with the tailored refinement of an editorial publication.

The aesthetic philosophy draws heavily from high-contrast brutalist framing, cinematographic sports campaigns, and precision technical HUDs. 

- **Key Aesthetics:** Monolithic dark surfaces, hyper-condensed monumental type scales, razor-sharp technical containers, dynamic 12-degree kinetic diagonals, and strategic strikes of vivid laser red.
- **Target Audience:** High-performing athletes, executives, and dedicated fitness purists who view physical conditioning as an art and a discipline.
- **Emotional Response:** Invigorated focus, elite exclusivity, discipline, mechanical precision, and raw physical power.

## Colors

The palette relies on absolute darkness punctured by high-output photon red, echoing stadium lights, carbon fiber chassis, and heart-rate peak zones.

### Core Swatches
- **Base Canvas (`#050505`):** Deep Obsidian Black for full-viewport grounding.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#0B0D0F`):** Carbon Black for primary section cards and structural framing.
- **Surface Tier 1 (`#111418`):** Deep Graphite for interactive blocks and secondary panels.
- **Surface Tier 2 (`#1A1E22`):** Medium Graphite for active hover states, table rows, and borders.
- **Border / Structural (`#30343A`):** Steel Gray for technical hair lines, grids, and division markers.
- **Primary Text (`#F5F5F5`):** Crisp Off-White for pure contrast and surgical legibility.
- **Muted Text (`#969AA0`):** Secondary Cool Gray for metrics metadata, footnotes, and unit indicators.
- **Action Primary (`#FF1820`):** Vivid Laser Red for mission-critical CTAs, heart-rate zones, and active statuses.
- **Action Heavy (`#E50914`):** Athletic Red for badges, state indicators, and progress bars.
- **Crimson Shadow (`#720008`):** Dark Crimson for deep ambient glows, badge backdrops, and gradient stops.

### Usage Hierarchy
Maintain an 85/10/5 ratio: 85% deep carbon neutrals, 10% crisp white text and structural steel lines, and 5% laser red impact hits. Laser Red must never be diluted with pastel variations; it exists solely to command immediate ocular focus.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an aggressive contrast between the towering vertical cadence of sports editorial headlines and the neutral engineering of modern grotesque body type.

- **Headlines (Anton):** Rendered in all-caps. Anton provides immense presence, athletic density, and tight vertical rhythm reminiscent of iconic sportswear posters. Tight line heights eliminate dead space.
- **Data & Micro Technical Labels (Barlow Condensed):** Monospaced-leaning, high-density condensed grotesque. Used for telemetry readouts, timestamps, badges, and uppercase metadata. Always tracked wide (`letter-spacing: 0.14em` to `0.2em`).
- **Body & Continuous Copy (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral workhorse. Balances the extreme personality of the headlines, ensuring seamless reading across dense training routines, workout regimens, and specifications.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric 12-column editorial grid structured on razor-sharp architectural guidelines.

### Breakpoints & Canvas
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Dynamic stack layout with full-bleed imagery and compact horizontal stat scrollers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Asymmetric column pair shifts (e.g., 5-col image, 3-col data).
- **Desktop (≥1200px):** 12 columns, max-width `1600px`, `margin: 4rem`, `gutter: 2.5rem`. Split hero panes, off-center focal points, and massive overlapping typography blocks.

### Kinetic Diagonals
Break traditional horizontal sections using a 12-degree angled kinetic ribbon cut (`transform: skewY(-12deg)` or CSS `clip-path: polygon(0 5vw, 100% 0, 100% calc(100% - 5vw), 0 100%)`). These angled transitions host continuous ticker tape marathons, partner marks, or performance metrics.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through pitch-black surface tiers, industrial hairline borders, and tactical crimson luminescences rather than traditional soft drop shadows.

- **Ground Zero (Canvas):** Pure `#050505`. The foundational infinite floor.
- **Layer 01 (Structural Panels):** Solid `#0B0D0F` bordered with 1px `#30343A` at 40% opacity.
- **Layer 02 (Interactive Units & Cards):** `#111418` with 1px solid `#1A1E22`. Upon hover, the border flashes to 1px `#FF1820` accompanied by a localized directional glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(255, 24, 32, 0.35)`.
- **Topmost Layer (Header & Overlays):** Dark glassmorphic execution (`background: rgba(5, 5, 5, 0.75)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, bottom hairline border `1px solid rgba(48, 52, 58, 0.5)`).
- **Vignetting & Photography:** High-contrast athletic images are treated with a dark radial gradient vignette masking into `#050505`, ensuring live metrics and copy sit on high-contrast foundations.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly sharp (`roundedness: 0`). Elements have 0px border radius.

- **Brutal Geometry:** Every card, button, image container, and input field features hard, unapologetic 90-degree corners.
- **Chamfered Technical Notches:** Specialized hero cards and technical status chips employ a 45-degree corner cut (`clip-path: polygon(0 0, calc(100% - 8px) 0, 100% 8px, 100% 100%, 0 100%)`) to communicate military and aeronautic performance hardware styling.
- **Divider Lines:** 1px hairline rules using Steel Gray (`#30343A`) to build modular cockpit-style structural boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Button:** Razor-sharp rectangular bounds, solid Vivid Laser Red (`#FF1820`) background, Crisp White text (`#F5F5F5`), set in `label-caps` (Anton or Barlow Condensed, uppercase, tracked). Includes an inline 45-degree arrow icon. Hover: transitions background to pure white `#F5F5F5` and text to `#050505`, with a crimson box glow.
- **Secondary Ghost Button:** 1px solid `#30343A` border, transparent background, `#F5F5F5` text. Hover: border flashes to `#FF1820`, text shifts to `#FF1820`, subtle red ambient background tint (`rgba(255, 24, 32, 0.06)`).

### Metric Counter Blocks
Massive headline figures in Anton (`stat-counter` scale). The metric value is rendered in `#F5F5F5` with the delta indicator (+12%, BPM, WATTS) rendered in `#FF1820` using `technical-mono`. Set inside a `#0B0D0F` chassis container with a 1px `#30343A` top border.

### Status Chips & Badges
Rectangular technical tags with 0px radius. Dark background (`#111418`), border `1px solid rgba(255, 24, 32, 0.3)`. Text rendered in `technical-mono` uppercase with a 6px pulsing circular dot indicator in `#FF1820`.

### Cards & Membership Surfaces
Monolithic black cards (`#0B0D0F`) framed with 1px `#30343A`.
- **Tier "Elite/Black" Tiers:** Features an active 1px full border in `#FF1820` with a top-right chamfered corner tag reading "PRO ATHLETE ACCESS".
- **Internal Spacing:** Generous `space-xl` padding. Content is organized with high-contrast structural separator lines (`#1A1E22`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Sharp corner boxes, background `#111418`, border `1px solid #30343A`, text `#F5F5F5`. Placeholder text in `#969AA0`. Focus state: border turns into 1px `#FF1820` with zero radius change and no external rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square indicators (0px radius). Checked state displays a solid `#FF1820` fill with a pure black checkmark or dot.

### Diagonal Ribbon Banners
Full-bleed angled ribbons set between sections at a -12 degree angle, featuring infinitely scrolling marquee text in `headline-md` Anton uppercase ("STRENGTH • DISCIPLINE • AGILITY • RECOVERY") in `#050505` on a solid `#FF1820` or `#F5F5F5` tape.
